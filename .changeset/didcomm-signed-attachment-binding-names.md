The `didcomm_signed_attachment` binding method types now have neutral names, such as `DidCommSignedAttachmentBindingMethods` and `DidCommSignedAttachmentOfferBindingMethod`, shared by the W3C VC SD-JWT and `dc+sd-jwt` credential formats. The old `W3cV2SdJwt` names still work but are deprecated.
